How to Play

Objective: Defeat all opponent's dinosaurs.

Turns: Both players secretly choose a move. Higher SPD goes first.

Moves: Strike (basic attack), Pass (do nothing), Swap (switch active dino), and unique abilities.

Abilities: Each dinosaur has special moves with cooldowns.

Cooldowns: After using an ability, you must wait the shown number of turns before using it again.

Stats: ⚡Speed determines turn order. ⚔️Attack and 🛡️Defense affect damage.

Status Effects: Bleed (🩸), Venom (🖤), Wound (🩶), Dodge (💨), Stun (💫), Regeneration (🩷), etc.

Swapping: You can switch to a backup dinosaur. Some dinosaurs prevent swapping.

Winning: Reduce all opponent HP to 0.

AI Mode: The computer opponent chooses randomly.

Use the "Lock In" button to confirm your move. Then watch the resolution step by step.
